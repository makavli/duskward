# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release: the whole journey south.

- **Eight evenings**, each a place with its own sky, country and sound, and one thing to learn:
  The Saltmarsh (a squall to slip under), Hop Gardens (wirework, and a falcon that stoops twice), Market Town
  (bonfire-night rockets, and warm air over the roofs), The Wind Farm (turning blades and a river of wind), Estuary
  Fog (stay gathered, follow the calls), City of Bridges (bridges no falcon flies under, and gulls a ripple turns
  away), Chalk Cliffs (sea arches, and two falcons out of the sun), The Long Reeds (midwinter, snow, and every flock
  dancing together).
- **The eight** have names. The flock you bring to a roost sets out with you the next evening; one of the eight
  taken by a falcon, left behind or lost to the cold is gone for the rest of the journey. The ending counts who came
  home.
- **The journey's map**: the road south, three feathers an evening (24 in all), the great flock that follows you,
  and any evening to fly again.
- **A save**, written after every evening, on the desktop and in the browser.
- **One button**: hold to gather, tap to ripple, point to steer. Mouse, keyboard or gamepad.
- For Windows (`Duskward.exe`, one file) and for the browser (`Duskward-web.zip`).
